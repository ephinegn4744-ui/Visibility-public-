📋 Scholarship Application Checklist

  • Check the eligibility requirements.
  • Confirm the application deadline.
  • Prepare academic transcripts.
  • Prepare recommendation letters if required.
  • Write your personal statement or essays.
  • Check whether tuition, accommodation and travel are covered.
  • Submit your application through the official website.
⚠️ Important: Scholarship information and deadlines can change. Always confirm the current requirements on the official organization's website. Never pay an unknown person who promises to guarantee a scholarship.
